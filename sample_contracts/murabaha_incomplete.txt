MURABAHA FINANCING AGREEMENT
Document ID: MUR-INCOMPLETE-001

Clause 1 — Parties

This agreement is made between Amanah Finance Ltd., referred to as
“the Bank,” and Noor Trading LLC, referred to as “the Customer.”

Clause 2 — Asset

The asset covered by this agreement is one Model ZX-200 industrial
packaging machine, serial number ZX200-45872, supplied by Gulf
Machinery Ltd.

Clause 3 — Murabaha Sale Price

The Bank sells the asset to the Customer for a total sale price of
AED 110,000.

Clause 4 — Payment Terms

The Customer shall pay the sale price in 22 monthly installments of
AED 5,000. The first installment is due on 1 November 2026.

Clause 5 — Acceptance

The Customer accepts the asset, total sale price, and payment terms
described in this agreement.
