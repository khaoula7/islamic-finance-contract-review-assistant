MURABAHA FINANCING AGREEMENT
Document ID: MUR-AMBIGUOUS-001

Clause 1 — Parties

This agreement is made between Amanah Finance Ltd., referred to as
“the Bank,” and Noor Trading LLC, referred to as “the Customer.”

Clause 2 — Asset

The financing relates to industrial equipment selected by the Customer
from an approved supplier.

Clause 3 — Acquisition

The Bank will arrange the purchase of the equipment and will sell it
to the Customer after the required acquisition procedures have been
completed.

Clause 4 — Cost and Profit

The original cost of the equipment is AED 100,000. The Bank shall add
an agreed Murabaha profit.

Clause 5 — Price and Payment

The final sale price and installment schedule shall be stated in a
payment schedule agreed between the Bank and the Customer.

Clause 6 — Acceptance

The Customer accepts the general terms of this agreement.
