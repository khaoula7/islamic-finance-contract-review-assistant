MURABAHA FINANCING AGREEMENT
Document ID: MUR-COMPLETE-001

Clause 1 — Parties

This Murabaha Financing Agreement is made between Amanah Finance Ltd.,
referred to as “the Bank,” and Noor Trading LLC, referred to as
“the Customer.”

Clause 2 — Asset

The asset covered by this agreement is one Model ZX-200 industrial
packaging machine, serial number ZX200-45872, supplied by Gulf
Machinery Ltd.

Clause 3 — Bank Purchase and Ownership

At the Customer’s request, the Bank purchased the asset from Gulf
Machinery Ltd. on 15 October 2026. The Bank acquired ownership and
possession of the asset before entering into the Murabaha sale with
the Customer.

Clause 4 — Original Cost and Profit

The Bank’s original purchase cost was AED 100,000.

The Bank disclosed a Murabaha profit of AED 10,000 to the Customer.

Clause 5 — Murabaha Sale Price

The Bank sells the asset to the Customer for a total Murabaha sale
price of AED 110,000.

Clause 6 — Payment Terms

The Customer shall pay the sale price in 22 monthly installments of
AED 5,000. The first installment is due on 1 November 2026, and each
subsequent installment is due on the first day of the following month.

Clause 7 — Acceptance

The Customer confirms that the asset, original cost, profit amount,
sale price, and payment schedule have been clearly disclosed and
accepted.
